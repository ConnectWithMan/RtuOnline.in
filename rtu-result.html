About RTU Result – Rajasthan Technical University

Rajasthan Technical University (RTU), Kota is the apex technical university of Rajasthan. It conducts semester examinations for thousands of B.Tech students. The RTU result is declared on the official university portal and can be accessed using a student's roll number.

The RTU semester result is published as a detailed grade sheet that includes subject-wise marks, grades, SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average), and CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average). Students must score a minimum SGPA to be promoted to the next semester as per RTU regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions about RTU Result

How can I check my RTU Result 2026?

You can check your RTU Result 2026 by clicking on the relevant semester link on this page. You will be redirected to the official RTU portal at rtu.sumsraj.com where you need to enter your roll number to download your grade sheet.

What details are required to check the RTU Result?

To view your RTU result, you generally need your examination Roll Number, Name, and Mother's Name as per your university records.

When is the RTU Result declared?

Rajasthan Technical University typically declares the RTU semester result within 45–60 days after the examination ends. Revaluation results are declared separately. Check this page regularly for the latest updates on RTU result announcements.

What is a grade sheet in RTU Result?

A grade sheet is an official document issued by RTU that shows subject-wise marks, grades, SGPA, and CGPA obtained by a student in a particular semester. You can download your RTU grade sheet from the official portal using the links provided above.

Can I apply for revaluation after the RTU Result is declared?

Yes. After the main RTU result is declared, students who are not satisfied with their marks can apply for revaluation or re-checking through the official RTU portal within the notified deadline. Revaluation results are published separately on the RTU portal.

Where is the official RTU Result portal?
