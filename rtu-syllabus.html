About Rajasthan Technical University (RTU)

Rajasthan Technical University (RTU) is a state technical university located in Kota, Rajasthan, established in 2006. It is the affiliating university for over 200 engineering and technical colleges in Rajasthan. RTU is known for its rigorous engineering curriculum, examination system, and efforts to align its syllabus with modern industry requirements and the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020.

Frequently Asked Questions – RTU Syllabus

Where can I download RTU Syllabus? +
You can download the RTU Syllabus for all B.Tech branches directly from RTU Online (rtuonline.in/rtu-syllabus). Select your branch and semester above to get the PDF download link instantly.
Which branches does RTU offer B.Tech in? +
RTU offers B.Tech in Computer Science (CS), Civil Engineering (CE), Mechanical Engineering (ME), Information Technology (IT), Electrical Engineering (EE), Electronics & Communication (ECE), Electronics & Instrumentation (EIC), and Production & Industrial Engineering (PI). The 1st and 2nd semesters are common for all branches.
How many semesters are there in RTU B.Tech? +
RTU B.Tech is a 4-year programme comprising 8 semesters. The first two semesters (1st Year) are common for all branches, while semesters 3 to 8 are branch-specific with specialised subjects and practicals.
Is the RTU syllabus the same for all affiliated colleges? +
Yes, all colleges affiliated to Rajasthan Technical University (RTU) follow the same prescribed syllabus. The theory curriculum, practicals, and examination scheme are uniform across all RTU-affiliated engineering colleges in Rajasthan.
How often does RTU update its syllabus? +
RTU periodically revises its B.Tech syllabus to align with industry requirements and the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020. Major curriculum overhauls happen every few years, with minor subject-level updates occurring more frequently. Always refer to the latest syllabus available on this page.
