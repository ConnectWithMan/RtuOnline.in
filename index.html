Exam Results & Revaluation

Stay updated on result announcement timelines, copy revaluation procedures, and score checking protocols.

Checking Your Scores

Students can check semester grades through the official RTU online portal. Main exams, back paper evaluations, and revaluation scores are posted periodically throughout the academic year.

If you suspect a marking error, RTU offers a formal path to request a copy view or complete revaluation of your answer books. Be sure to review the application timelines to submit requests before deadline dates.

Interactive SGPA to Percentage Converter

Under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) implemented by RTU, student performance is graded in Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA).

Convert your SGPA/CGPA to equivalent percentage points instantly using the official conversion formula: Percentage = SGPA × 10.
